Getting started

  • Sign in with your email and password. Use Forgot password? if you cannot remember it. The first person to create an account becomes Super Administrator; everyone else starts as Read Only until the Super Administrator assigns a role under Users.
  • Open My profile (your picture, top right) to add a profile picture, change your display name and turn on two-step verification.
  • The bell shows your notifications: new discussion messages, beneficiaries waiting for review, reports and events starting tomorrow.

Roles in short

  • Super Administrator: everything, including users, departments, categories and backups.
  • Organization Administrator: beneficiaries, projects, events, reports, approvals and the audit log.
  • Department Manager: their own department's beneficiaries and events; approves new registrations.
  • Data Entry Officer: registers and imports beneficiaries; records go to Pending Review until a manager approves them.
  • Project Officer / M&E Officer: indicators, follow-up visits, participants, attendance and reports.
  • Donor: sees only approved reports and documents shared with donors, and the discussions shared with donors.
  • Read Only: views approved information.

Beneficiaries

  • Register: Beneficiaries → Register. Fill the personal, participation and location sections. Type a new place in the location boxes if it does not exist yet.
  • Duplicates: if someone with the same phone number or the same name already exists, the system shows them and asks you to confirm before registering again.
  • Review queue: managers approve or reject records waiting for review.
  • Import: upload a CSV file (download the template first). Imported records are checked for duplicates.
  • Sensitive data: only record emergency contact or accessibility needs when it is needed and the person has given consent. Some departments (for example Reproductive Health Education) are restricted to authorised staff.
  • Offline: with no internet, new registrations are saved on your device and sent automatically when you are back online. Open the system once with internet first so it can be used offline.

Events, attendance and projects

  • Create trainings and seminars with dates and times. Their status (Planned, In Progress, Completed) updates automatically from the dates.
  • Programs → Participants: register participants, mark completion and enter pre/post-test scores. Programs → Attendance: mark attendance for each day.
  • Programs → Indicators: set targets; verified achievements are entered after checking.
  • Programs → Documents: upload many files at once. Photos and videos are stored on Cloudinary and open inside the system; documents are stored privately.

Reports

  • Reports shows live totals and totals by location. Create a report for donors, then an administrator approves it. Use Download PDF to print or save a report with the Terraviva logo.

Need help?

Contact your Super Administrator for account, role or department changes.
