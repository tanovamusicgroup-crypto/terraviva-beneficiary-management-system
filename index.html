About Terraviva

Terraviva is a non-profit organization headquartered in Ilemela District, Mwanza Region, Tanzania. Our slogan is Protecting Nature, Empowering Communities. We address the challenges of climate change and strengthen community resilience through four programs.

Sustainable Agriculture

Climate-smart agriculture, soil health, agroforestry and sustainable livelihoods.

Youth and Women Empowerment

Leadership, skills, livelihoods and opportunities for young people and women.

Environmental Conservation

Biodiversity, restoration, waste management and tree planting.

Reproductive Health Education

Reproductive health education and menstrual hygiene awareness.
